1

No tests while the match is live

Once the match is live, a test press is a real pause, so both tests are disabled.

2

The archive keeps every real press too

Every press in every match, with its timings and outcome, kept for good and exportable, not just the tests.

3

The coach sees the result on the booth's beacon

The beacon flashes for "press your button now" and spins briefly when the press reached the panel for the right team. That way the coach needs no screen.
